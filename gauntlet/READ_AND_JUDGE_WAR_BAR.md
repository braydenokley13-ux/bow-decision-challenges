# The read-and-judge war — the bar, written before the designs

**Filed 19 Aug 2026, before any candidate existed.** The order matters: the last war's brief supplied a vehicle in its own *known traps* section and three of four Module 2 designs came back wearing it. A bar written after the designs is a bar tuned to the designs.

## What is being contested

**One World that closes NYSED 1.4 and 1.5.** No other build in this product closes two objectives in Topic 1, and Topic 1 is 1 of 6 with both flagship Worlds living in it.

It must produce, for **every** student who plays:

**`judge-a-claim`** · BOW-B5 · `read-and-judge` · `explanationRequired: true` · *full* on 1.4, *partial* on 1.5
> *Decide whether information about a product or service can be trusted, and say what makes it credible or not.*
> Compare sources that disagree · Identify who benefits from the claim · Act on the more credible one.
> Misconceptions: **"A review is evidence"** · **"More detail means more true"**

**`notice-influence`** · BOW-B6 · `choose-under-pressure` · `explanationRequired: true` · *full* on 1.5
> *Identify what is pushing a spending decision — friends, an ad, an app, a deadline, the way the price is shown — and decide anyway.*
> Encounter pressure to spend · Name it · Make a decision that accounts for it.
> Misconceptions: **"Ads don't work on me"** · **"Urgency is information"**

**Both have zero authored evidence requirements.** A design therefore proposes its rows *and* the acts that produce them, together — because the standing failure in this repository is rows authored away from the world that has to produce them, a world built against a stale version, and a court scoring a third. Proposed rows are proposals. What ships is decided after the war and stamped.

## Automatic disqualifications

A design is out, not marked down, if:

1. **One obviously virtuous answer.** If "be suspicious of everything" or "always distrust the ad" scores 5, the world measures compliance. Distrust must be able to be *wrong* in this world, and at least one sceptical student must be able to score badly for a nameable reason.
2. **Every document is truthful.** Six shipped and designed worlds have this property; Ferro's $9 fee is *hidden*, not *false*. A world about credibility in which nothing is unreliable is a world about reading carefully. Something must actually not be true, and the student must find out.
3. **The mechanic is workbench, timeline, or inspection desk with new art.** Distribute-into-containers, place-on-a-time-axis, bind-a-line-to-a-question. A fourth family is what the portfolio needs; three of these already carry six worlds.
4. **Reflex scores as reasoning.** A tap sequence a student can produce without engaging with who benefits, or with what the two sources actually disagree about, must not produce a top level on any row.
5. **The written rows can be satisfied by restating the prompt.** `explanationRequired` is `true` on both competencies. An explanation row a student clears by echoing the question is worse than no row, because it reports mastery.
6. **Accessibility destroys the construct.** Whatever a screen reader or keyboard user gets must let them make *the same judgement*, not a simplified one. "Read the two sources aloud in order" is not equivalent to seeing them side by side if side-by-side is where the disagreement lives — say what the equivalent is.

## What is scored

| | |
|---|---|
| **Coverage, against the spine** | Every proposed row routed to a named act, with the log event it lands in. Rows that cannot be routed are not coverage |
| **Can it tell thinking from reflex** | The specific tap sequence a non-thinking student produces, and what it scores. Name it, do not assert it cannot happen |
| **Is the fourth family real** | The hand-act, stated as a verb a person does. If it reduces to one of the three, say so |
| **Both shapes in one world** | `read-and-judge` and `choose-under-pressure` are different shapes. Does one world carry both honestly, or is it two acts stapled together |
| **Reading load** | Raw words, counted, against ~2,200 and ~24 minutes at this grade band. Measured, not estimated |
| **Does a 5–8 student want to play it** | Not "is it educational." Would they choose it |
| **What it costs to build** | New design-system primitives, new log events, new observer surface |
| **Motif** | The story. A motif that could be swapped for another without changing a single verb is a reskin, and the point of many motifs is that they are not |

## Rules of the run

- Designs are judged by readers who did not write them and who see **no designer reasoning** — the design as submitted, this bar, and the two competency definitions above.
- **All candidates may lose.** "The best of five" is not a result. A verdict that nothing here clears the bar is a real verdict and closes the question faster than shipping the least-bad one.
- Incumbency is worth nothing. Nothing is built in this family, so there is no incumbent — but neither does a design earn anything for being first.
- The war decides a *direction*. It does not author the rubric; it produces the candidate rows the rubric is then written from, in the file, stamped.
