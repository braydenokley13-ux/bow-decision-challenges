What should I teach next?

One gap stands out: Savings is a planned amount. 3 of the 13 with a usable result missed it — the other four parts of this skill held. The misconception behind it: savings is leftover money.

Seats 3, 6 and 12 — in both stories, savings took whatever remained after everything else was filled.

A 12-minute reteach: set the savings figure before anything else
  1. Show two plans that balance at the same total — one where savings was set first, one where it took what was left. Ask which student is more likely to hit the goal.
  2. Give one income and one list of costs. Everyone writes the savings line before touching any other line.
  3. Change one cost by a small amount and ask what has to move. The savings line is the one that must not.
