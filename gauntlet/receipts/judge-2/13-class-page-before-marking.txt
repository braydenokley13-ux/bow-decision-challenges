WHAT APPEARS MISUNDERSTOOD

Savings is leftover money

Savings is set to a deliberate figure before discretionary categories are filled, not as the remainder after them

Nobody's writing has been read yet, so there is nothing to quote. The counts above come from what students did, not from what they wrote.

TEACH NEXT

Set the savings figure before anything else

A student writes a savings amount down first, then builds the rest of the plan around it.

Show two plans that balance at the same total — one where savings was set first, one where it took what was left. Ask which student is more likely to hit the goal.
Give one income and one list of costs. Everyone writes the savings line before touching any other line.
Change one cost by a small amount and ask what has to move. The savings line is the one that must not.

About 12 minutes.

Why this class: 9 of 9 assessed students (100%) did not show “savings is a planned amount”.
