Mo has cooked at Salt and Smoke for two summers and never had a booth of his own. The market runs for four Saturdays. There is $1,900 in the truck's account, and the permit and the booth are paid before anybody sells a plate.

Mo does the cooking. You handle the money, and you have four Saturdays to get it right.

Where do you set up?

The organiser prices a booth by the crowd that walks past it. Take one. The same four Saturdays at every booth. What changes is how many walk past yours. The last one is a range: nobody can say how many will come for the fireworks.
