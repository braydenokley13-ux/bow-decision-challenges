WEEK 3 PAYS AVERY IN CASH.

Rent and the basics leave the account on schedule. Then Avery covers a teammate’s shift and is handed $150. It is not in the plan and it cannot go in — and three things want it before Sunday.

Team shoes
$120
The pair Avery plays in are splitting along the side. Avery can keep playing in them. It hurts.
Away-game travel share
$60
Everyone on the bus pays their share. Avery already told the coach they were in.
A present for Avery's sister
$45
She turns eleven on Saturday.

$150
left of $150. What Avery keeps in hand does nothing for any of these three.
