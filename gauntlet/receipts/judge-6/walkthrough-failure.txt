Judge 6 note. This is `e2e/walkthrough.spec.ts` copied verbatim into my own test dir, cut
to the 1366 size and given a 240s per-test budget instead of 180s, run against a class
service and app that were already up. It produced 15 of ~25 screenshots and then waited
240 seconds for a button that the product is correct not to be showing.

Three independent runs, two of them through `npm run walkthrough` unmodified, stopped at
the same screenshot: 08c-deposit-deadline.

Cause: walkthrough.spec.ts presses TO_DEPOSIT on the Week 3 screen without first funding a
claim and giving a reason. e2e/flow.ts::playSeasonWeeks — which every assertion test uses —
does `.claims__list button` then `.claims__why button` then TO_DEPOSIT. The walkthrough
does not. Adding those two clicks to the same sequence in
gauntlet/receipts/judge-6/specs/basketball.spec.ts carries it past this point in ~3s and
through all 25 screens in 37s.

The walkthrough test.skip()s unless WALKTHROUGH_OUT is set, so no CI build runs it.
