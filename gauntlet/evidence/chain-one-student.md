## What is deliberately absent

`notObserved`: 0 entries.

*Incomplete* above is not a low score and is not a failure. Where the run cannot say, the
product says nothing: an unread written explanation is a fact about a teacher's marking
backlog, and reporting it as a zero would make it a fact about a child.

### The rows that wait on a person, by design

Every requirement whose `kind` is `explanation` is scored by a teacher and never by the
product. That is why two of the three competencies above are still open on a run where the
student did everything: the machine has finished and the marking has not.

- `sort-by-need-want-goal.er4` — *Says what made one claim matter more* · required · currently **unmarked**
- `plan-within-income.er5` — *Explains the trade-off made* · required · currently **unmarked**
- `adapt-a-plan.er5` — *Explains what they protected and why* · optional · currently **unmarked**

So a run can be complete and a competency still open. The product does not close that gap by
guessing, and there is no path in it by which a machine scores a child's writing.
